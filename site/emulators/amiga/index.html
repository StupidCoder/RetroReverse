Compatibility

Capture records bitplane and sprite scanout, Copper register writes, blitter writes and disk DMA. Follow bitplane words and palette registers back to writers within the captured interval. Replay reconstructs the modeled scanout buffer; it does not simulate individual DMA bus slots. Chip RAM uses its native addresses; slow RAM is mapped to 0x80000, custom registers to 0x100000 and RGBA scanout to 0x110000 in the inspector.
