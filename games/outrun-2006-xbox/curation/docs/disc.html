The disc is a standard Xbox XISO carrying roughly 3,400 files around a 3.7 MB executable. Almost everything else follows one convention: a .sz file is a bare zlib stream — two magic bytes, then compressed data, no container of any kind.

One decompressor for everything

A single reader routine serves every .sz on the disc. It pulls 64 KB chunks into two ping-pong buffers and inflates in flight through a statically linked zlib, so no asset ever exists fully decompressed in main memory. The reader even supports seeking forward inside a stream it cannot rewind: it inflates into a 64-byte scratch buffer and throws the bytes away until it reaches the target offset.

What lives where

/Cars

The sixty-three vehicle models — see The cars.

/Sprani

Sprite-animation banks for everything two-dimensional: menu screens, loading cards, rank screens, and one speedometer face per car (ani_SPRANI_METER_246GTS and friends — the HUD loads the meter matching the car being driven).

/Stage and /BK

Per-course world data: geometry, environments, animations and objects for each of the fifteen courses, keyed by four-letter course codes (BEAC is the opening beach).

/Scripts/bin

Compiled per-course binaries named for their role: a Req_… table per course (with _r twins for the reversed layouts), goal tables, and TracksideCameras.bin.

/mv, and the audio streams

Movies ship as XMV; the title attract movie is decoded by the game's own codec on the CPU, using MMX integer SIMD. Music and speech ship as WMA and ADX streams; the WMA decoder likewise runs on the CPU with SSE.

/Driver, /Chr, /OCP, /Ghosts

Driver and character models, and the ghost-lap store.
