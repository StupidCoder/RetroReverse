None of the fortress's moving architecture uses an object slot. The map cells never change; the character glyphs they display are redefined at runtime — and because solidity is decided by scanning a glyph's eight pixel bytes (any non-zero byte is solid), redefining one character opens or closes every cell that uses it, everywhere on the map, at once. A blanked barrier is genuinely open air for everything simultaneously.

Energy barriers

Two barrier groups run on independent timers, started half a cycle apart so their flashes alternate. A barrier glyph is blank except during the single 8-frame window when its timer wraps — a brief, periodic lethal flash rather than a standing wall. On the first level the barriers form diagonal scissor gates across the cavern passages, and flying into a lit gate does not kill: it teleports the helicopter to one of four cavern drop points. The gates are the level's transport system — hover in the funnel and let the flash take you.

Laser grids

The grid corridors tile four glyphs in alternation. Every 128 frames all four are blanked and each is independently re-lit with 50% probability from the noise generator. A lit segment is solid and lethal, a dark one is open air — there is no pattern to learn; passage through a grid is genuinely probabilistic.

The sweeping walls

Corridor bands repeat runs of four wall characters of which exactly one is solid at a time; stepping the phase marches a 6-column wall section through the corridor. The direction of the march is toggled by — of all things — every press of the fire button, anywhere on the map: each shot reverses the sweep.

The reactor gates

The reactor chamber has two gate walls, one glyph each. At every life start both definitions are cleared and one of the two — chosen by the sign bit of the noise oscillator — is filled solid. Each life, one gate is a wall and the other is invisible and passable, at random. Destroying the fort core blanks both for the escape. (The shipped glyphs for these two characters are leftover font digits — they are overwritten before play ever begins, so they are never seen in the game.)
